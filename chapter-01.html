Evolution of CSR in India

Pre 2014

Philanthropic Era

Voluntary charity by Tata, Birla, Bajaj driven by Gandhian trusteeship.

2014

Legislative Era

Enactment of Companies Act 2013 (Sec 135). Mandatory compliance.

2021

Impact Era

Amendments mandating formal impact assessments for large projects.

CSR Communication

Five Pillars Framework

Pillar 1:
Corporate Entity

Examples: Tata Steel, HDFC Bank, JSPL, SAIL, NMDC, Coal India, Axis Bank, Yes Bank, SBI

Pillar 2:
Implementation Agency

Two implementation modes: Direct (company's own CSR team) | Partnered (via NGO/foundation).

Pillar 3:
Initiative Type

Examples: Education, Health, Environment, Livelihood, Women Empowerment, Infrastructure

Pillar 4:
Impact & Outcome

Domains: Educational, Health, Environmental, Economic, Social, Infrastructural gains

Pillar 5:
Communication Strategy
(Focal Variable)

Channels: Verbal, Print, Electronic, Social Media | Language: Tribal dialects vs Hindi/English | Feedback: Two-way grievance mechanisms

Significance of the Study

Academic

Fills the research gap by applying communication theories to rural/tribal contexts in India.

Policy

Provides empirical evidence for the Ministry of Corporate Affairs to refine CSR reporting guidelines.

Practical

Offers an actionable framework for corporates and NGOs to improve beneficiary engagement.
