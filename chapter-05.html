Data Collection

Primary Sources

Structured Surveys

Bilingual (Hindi/English) Likert-scale questionnaires.

In-depth Interviews

Semi-structured discussions with stakeholders.

Field Observations

Conducted across 10 districts over 6 months.

Secondary Sources

National CSR Portal

Expenditure data from Ministry of Corporate Affairs.

Corporate Reports

Annual Reports, Business Responsibility Reports (BRSR).

Academic Databases

Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar.

Data Analysis Techniques

SPSS v26.0 — Quantitative

Shows the average and spread of the data.

Descriptive Stats

Checks if CG and Jharkhand are significantly different.

Independent t-test

Checks if a communication channel works differently in each state.

Two-Way ANOVA

Checks if channel choice and environmental awareness are related.

Chi-Square

Predicts YES/NO outcomes (e.g., did education improve?).

Logistic Regression

Predicts a score (e.g., livelihood improvement) using multiple factors.

Multiple Regression

NVivo v12 — Qualitative

Converts spoken interviews into written text.

Audio Transcription

Groups similar ideas into themes (e.g., 'trust', 'communication gap').

Thematic Coding

Creates folders to store themes for organised analysis.

Node Generation

Checks if people are positive, negative, or neutral.

Sentiment Analysis

Counts the most repeated words in interviews.

Word Frequency

Ethical Considerations

Informed Consent

Written or recorded verbal consent obtained before all surveys and interviews.

Anonymity

Personal identifiers removed. Beneficiaries assured that negative feedback would not impact aid.

Data Security

Raw data securely stored offline. Aggregate data reported to prevent triangulation of individuals.

Limitations of the Study

Challenge in isolating individual corporate impact in regions where multiple companies operate simultaneously.

Logistical dependency on company/NGO coordination to access secure remote tribal areas.

Nuance loss during translation from local tribal dialects (Gondi/Santhali) to Hindi/English.

Cross-sectional data collection prevents longitudinal measurement of impact evolution.

Delimitations

Geographical

Confined strictly to Chhattisgarh and Jharkhand.

Sectoral

Limited to Education, Health, Environment, and Livelihood.

Stakeholder Scope

Focuses on direct beneficiaries; excludes general public perception.

Variable Focus

Focuses exclusively on the communication aspect, not financial auditing.

Theoretical Scope

Bound by the newly proposed Five-Pillar Framework.

Regulatory Framework

Evaluated strictly under the parameters of Companies Act, 2013.
